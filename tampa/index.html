Keeping Your Tampa HVAC System Running Longer

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and schedule a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still inexpensive.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners last 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and starting to struggle, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and much easier on you than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A capacitor that's starting to fail is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, neglected for a season, can take out the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone now so help is one call away as soon as something seems off in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
