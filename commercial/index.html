Safe Installation

Professional Installation and Documentation

A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can create carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and expensive water damage from clogged condensate lines. Those are real risks for a commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Every installation and inspection we perform covers heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. Every account gets a documentation binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and auditors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent visit schedule.

Emergency Service

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

When a commercial HVAC system fails, the clock starts immediately. Kitchens overheat, retail floors empty out, server rooms heat up, and tenants start calling. Calls to our commercial line get a technician rolling fast, anywhere in Delray Beach and the surrounding area.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: our technicians have seen them all on rooftop and split systems. The priority is diagnosing the fault and getting heating or cooling back fast. After that, we put maintenance and monitoring in place so it doesn't happen again, and every response is fully documented for your records.

Ongoing Protection

Scheduled HVAC Maintenance for Commercial Properties

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment is preventing failures before they happen. Delray Beach HVAC offers customized scheduled maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, coil cleaning and condensate line clearing, filter changes with MERV ratings matched to the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant charge and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal preparation for peak demand throughout the year. Each program is tailored to the property's specific equipment, usage patterns, and budget requirements.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints.
