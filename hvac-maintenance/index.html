Why Professional HVAC Maintenance Matters

There is a short list of things homeowners can handle: changing filters on time, keeping the outdoor coil clear, leaving registers open, and glancing at the drain. The rest takes instruments, because the problems that leave you stranded look perfectly normal. A capacitor that has lost 20 percent of its rating looks the same as a new one. Low charge shows up only in superheat and subcooling readings. Cracked heat exchangers, weak flame signals, marginal pressure switches, and amp draws closing in on the breaker's limit are all invisible until measured, and all of them cost less to fix the season before they fail.

How to Prevent HVAC Maintenance Problems

A little routine maintenance goes a long way toward avoiding a mid-season breakdown. Follow these tips from our experienced technicians:

  • Give the condenser room to breathe and rinse its coil lightly each spring
  • Get your tune-ups on the calendar during mild weather, well before the rush
  • Test carbon monoxide detectors monthly if any of your equipment burns fuel
  • Note new sounds and longer run times; they are data your technician can use
  • Swap the filter every one to three months, the most valuable habit a homeowner can build
  • Resist closing vents in spare rooms; the system is designed with all registers open
  • Flush the condensate line every spring so algae cannot build up and clog it
